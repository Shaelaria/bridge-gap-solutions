04Contract delivery model

A defined process.

  1. 01

    Review the scope

    Analyze requirements, performance expectations, and compliance obligations.

  2. 02

    Qualify partners

    Qualify vendors and subcontractors where the solicitation permits.

  3. 03

    Compare pricing

    Obtain and evaluate quotes against the defined scope of work.

  4. 04

    Manage performance

    Coordinate schedules, quality control, and delivery.

  5. 05

    Track closeout

    Track final deliverables and closeout documentation.

Prime contractor accountability. Subcontractor coordination does not transfer our responsibility for contract delivery.
