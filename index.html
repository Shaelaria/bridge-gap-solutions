03Our process

How It Works

  1. 01

    An agency issues a solicitation

    A federal agency publishes a requirement and invites offers.

  2. 02

    We compete as the prime

    We submit an offer in our own name and sign the contract if awarded.

  3. 03

    We award subcontracts

    Where the contract permits, we hire qualified subcontracting companies to perform the work.

  4. 04

    We manage performance

    Schedule, quality, compliance, documentation, and invoicing — we manage the contract from award through completion.

How we qualify subcontractors

Our review before a company is named in an offer or receives a subcontract. Requirements vary by contract.

  • Not excluded from federal contracting (checked in SAM.gov)
  • A UEI or SAM.gov registration when the contract requires one
  • Any licenses required for the work and its location
  • Insurance and bonding that meet the contract's requirements
  • Relevant experience, references, and capacity for the scope

How we will manage an awarded contract

Our plan for each contract we win, from award through closeout:

  • One point of contact for the contracting officer
  • Written subcontracts that flow down required contract clauses
  • Offers structured to meet limitations on subcontracting on small-business set-asides
  • Schedule tracking, inspections, and quality-control checks against the contract requirements
  • Records, reports, invoicing, and closeout handled by Bridge Gap Solutions

Subcontracting never shifts responsibility. Bridge Gap Solutions remains the prime contractor, accountable to the government for the entire contract.
