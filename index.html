03Our process

How It Works

  1. 01

    Government needs work done

    A federal agency has a requirement and puts it out for contract.

  2. 02

    Bridge Gap pursues the contract

    We compete for it as the prime contractor.

  3. 03

    We hire a qualified company

    When subcontracting is allowed, a qualified company performs the work.

  4. 04

    We manage the contract

    Schedule, quality, documentation, and invoices — we manage delivery and the federal contract from start to finish.

Using subcontractors never shifts responsibility. Bridge Gap Solutions remains the prime contractor, accountable to the government for the entire contract.
