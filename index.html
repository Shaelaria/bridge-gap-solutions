03Our process

How It Works

  1. 01

    An agency issues a solicitation

    A federal agency publishes a requirement and invites offers.

  2. 02

    We compete as the prime

    We submit an offer in our own name and sign the contract if awarded.

  3. 03

    We award subcontracts

    Where the contract permits, we hire qualified subcontracting companies to perform the work.

  4. 04

    We manage performance

    We manage schedule, quality, compliance, records, and invoicing from award through closeout.

How we qualify subcontractors

Before a company is named in an offer or receives a subcontract, we confirm what that contract requires:

  • Not excluded from federal contracting (checked in SAM.gov)
  • A UEI or SAM.gov registration, if the contract requires one
  • Licenses required for the work and its location
  • Insurance and bonding that meet the contract's terms
  • Relevant experience, references, and capacity for the job

How we will manage each contract

Our commitments on every contract we win:

  • One point of contact for the contracting officer
  • Written subcontracts that pass along the contract's required terms
  • Subcontracted work kept within federal limits on small-business set-asides
  • Schedule tracking, inspections, and quality checks
  • Records, reports, invoices, and closeout handled by Bridge Gap Solutions

Subcontracting never shifts responsibility. Bridge Gap Solutions remains the prime contractor, accountable to the government for the entire contract.
